Meeting Your Responsibilities

Non-Profits, Endowments, and Foundations — overseeing endowments supporting non-profit organizations can be complex and daunting. As a board member, you have three fiduciary responsibilities: the duty of care, the duty of loyalty, and the duty of obedience. Each of these duties imposes obligations that must be met as state and common law mandate.
